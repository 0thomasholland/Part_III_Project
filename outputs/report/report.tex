I am deeply grateful to my supervisor, David Al-Attar, whose guidance has been central to everything in this project. David invested considerable time not only in directing the project but in teaching me the mathematical foundations it required, going well beyond what the project itself demanded so that I could engage with the material properly rather than superficially. His encouragement of independent thinking, and his skill in helping me balance genuine exploration with sensible scope management, made for excellent supervision throughout. His kindness and generosity with his time and expertise have been invaluable, and I am truly thankful for the opportunity to work with him.

Without the work of David and Daniel Heathcote on \citetitle{al-attarPySLFPPythonSea2025} and \citetitle{al-attarPygeoinfPythonLibrary2025}, none of this project would have been possible. Particular thanks to Helen for sharing her opinions on how to write mathematics correctly, and to Cara for proofreading the entire document several times over and providing invaluable feedback. They have helped enormously with keeping me motivated, generally sane, and have supported me immensely.

Finally, thanks are due to the fellow occupants of the Part III Geophysics lab: Peter Jackson, Candela Gil, Tom Devenish-Arzuza, Jiarui Sheng, and Oliver Tang, for being excellent company and willing sounding boards and suitable distractions throughout the year.
